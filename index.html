About Us

About Shengqi Yaoxing

Chongqing Shengqi Yaoxing Technology Development Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer integrating operations, R&D and manufacturing, engineering services (design, installation, commissioning) and after-sales support for stainless steel flagpoles, fully automatic hydraulic rising bollards, school anti-collision bollards, anti-terror road blockers, vehicle spike barriers and other security products.

The company operates multiple production lines with an experienced design and development team, and efficient management and technical staff. Built on solid capability, fair pricing and dependable service, we have established long-term partnerships with enterprises across China. From incoming inspection of materials and components to final testing before delivery, our technicians oversee every stage to ensure products meet standard requirements.

Our products are sold throughout China. We warmly welcome partners from all industries to visit our factory, inspect our work and discuss business.

Quality first, customers foremost, integrity as our creed — we strive to provide better products and genuinely satisfying service.
